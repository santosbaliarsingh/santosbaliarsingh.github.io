Biography

Dr. Santos Ku. Baliarsingh is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at KIIT Deemed to be University, Bhubaneswar, where he has taught and mentored students since December 2019. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT Bhubaneswar in 2020, working on metaheuristic-based classification algorithms for high-dimensional microarray data. His current research spans AI-generated text detection, video anomaly detection, and applied deep learning for healthcare — including a smartphone-based screening tool for oral potentially malignant disorders, funded by ICMR. He has authored 17 journal papers, 31 conference papers and a book chapter, and currently supervises four Ph.D. scholars.

Research

Research Areas

Core themes of my research programme. Swap in your own areas, icons and images.

📝

AI-Generated Text Detection

Fine-tuned transformer, RoBERTa-CNN and XLNet-CNN hybrid models for detecting AI-generated academic text, including low-resource-language and privacy-preserving detection settings.

🎥

Video Anomaly Detection

Weakly-supervised and caption-aware frameworks (CA-VAD, MSPL-VAD, PoinCLIP-VAD) for detecting anomalies in surveillance video.

🏥

Healthcare AI

Smartphone-based deep learning for early detection of oral potentially malignant disorders (ICMR-funded), plus diabetic retinopathy and multi-disease prediction models.

🧬

Bioinformatics & Metaheuristics

Metaheuristic-optimized extreme learning machines for high-dimensional microarray gene-expression and cancer classification — the focus of my Ph.D. thesis.

💬

NLP & Text Summarization

Abstractive summarization with GPT-2, T5, PEGASUS and BART, and agentic approaches to factuality-aware long-document summarization.

👁️

Computer Vision

Person and vehicle re-identification, pose estimation, and brain-tumour classification using CNN and transformer architectures.

📊

Applied Machine Learning

Predictive healthcare analytics, plant-disease detection, and comparative studies of ML approaches across real-world tabular datasets.

🛡️

Academic Integrity & Responsible AI

Studying the academic-integrity implications of generative AI adoption and building tools that support fair assessment.

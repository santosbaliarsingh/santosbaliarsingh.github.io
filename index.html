Biography

Dr. Santos Panda is an Assistant Professor working on natural language processing and responsible AI. Their research addresses the detection of AI-generated text, robustness of large language models, and the academic-integrity implications of generative AI adoption. They have contributed to peer-reviewed venues and collaborate with students on applied NLP projects.

Research

Research Areas

Core themes of my research programme. Swap in your own areas, icons and images.

🤖

Generative AI

Studying capabilities and failure modes of generative models across text, code and multimodal domains.

📝

AI-Generated Text Detection

Building detectors and benchmarks that distinguish human- and machine-authored text under adversarial conditions.

🧠

Large Language Models

Evaluating and fine-tuning LLMs (GPT-family, LLaMA, Mistral) for domain-specific reliability.

💬

Natural Language Processing

Text classification, information extraction, and low-resource language processing.

📊

Machine Learning

Supervised and self-supervised methods applied to text and tabular data.

🧬

Deep Learning

Transformer architectures, representation learning, and efficient training methods.

👁️

Computer Vision

Applied vision projects in collaboration with students, including document and image forensics.

🛡️

Responsible AI

Fairness, transparency and academic-integrity implications of AI adoption.
